What it does: draws two 100×100 canvases. The first is an rgba(100,150,200,0.5) fill read back twice (the probe of Figure 7a in the paper). The second adds a semi-transparent circle with anti-aliased edges to the same fill and is read back once directly and once after a drawImage round-trip through an OffscreenCanvas. The rules of Figure 7b then classify which canvas defense, if any, is active.
