OffscreenCanvas probe

Verifies that OffscreenCanvas extraction goes through the same per-frame budget and the same three modes as HTMLCanvasElement: 30 reads with a repaint before most of them, the fidelity of a paint fixed at its first read (reads 4-5 and 21-22 re-read the previous paint), and one shared budget for both canvas kinds.

Requires the default thresholds: coarse=3, block=20. Each page load starts with a fresh budget; reload to re-run.
